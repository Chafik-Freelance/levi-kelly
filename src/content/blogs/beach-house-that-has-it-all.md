If you are a fan of anything Florida related, you are going to love this place.

I've toured a lot of unique stays, but I recently visited my absolute favorite property I've featured in Florida so far. It’s a massive three-story beach home called **"It's About Time"**, located in the highly sought-after, private community of Seacrest Beach along Highway 30A.

Nestled perfectly between Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach, this spot is pure luxury. From the manicured walkways to the private security and pristine white sand beaches, it feels world-class. If you're bringing family or friends to the Gulf Coast and want the time of your life, this is the blueprint. Let's step inside.

## The Main House: Three Stories of Coastal Luxury

Right as you pull into your private driveway, you're greeted with a lineup of bicycles—the absolute best way to get around 30A. The hosts also provide an outdoor shower, a beach cart, and enough chairs, umbrellas, and toys for your entire group.

Once you step through the buffer of the laundry and drop-zone room, the house just opens up.

### First Floor: The Gathering Space

The first floor is designed to bring people together. It features a massive, U-shaped kitchen boasting beautiful natural light, floating shelves, a gas range, and plenty of counter space for cooking up big family meals.

This flows seamlessly into a bright dining area and a two-section living room. One side has a plush, comfortable couch setup, while the other side features four blue swivel chairs around a TV. There's also a bright, private bedroom with an attached subway-tiled bathroom right on the main floor—perfect for anyone who wants quick access to morning coffee.

Out the back doors, you’ll find an incredible enclosed patio under a canopy of trees, complete with a grill, outdoor dining table, and a gas fireplace. The best part? A walkway leads directly from the backyard to a quiet, private community park with cornhole and bocce ball.

### Second Floor: Private Retreats

Heading up the stairs, the second floor houses two spacious bedrooms. The front bedroom features its own TV, walk-in closet, private balcony patio, and an en-suite bathroom with a double vanity.

Walk down the hall and you'll find an even larger bedroom. This space includes an attached bathroom (complete with a bathtub), a bunk bed, a massive wall of windows, and a private balcony overlooking the backyard.

### Third Floor: The Ultimate Kids' Zone

The top level is where this house truly gets unique. Because you're a couple of floors away from the main kitchen, the third floor has its own kitchenette space complete with a mini-fridge, coffee maker, sink, and a cozy couch.

This level features the largest balcony space yet—perfect for sunbathing in total privacy. You'll also find the ultimate kids' bedroom up here, featuring four built-in bunk beds and an attached bathroom. What kid wouldn't want the top floor all to themselves?

## The Surprise in the Backyard: The Guest House

If a massive three-story home wasn't enough, there is an entirely separate guest house sitting off the back deck. It is a full one-bedroom, one-bathroom home with its own living room and kitchen setup.

This is an absolute game-changer if you're traveling with grandparents or someone in the group who just wants a little extra peace and privacy at the end of the day.

## Resort-Style Community Perks

The house is stunning, but the location is what pushes this property over the edge.

- **The Pool:** Literally across the street from the house is a massive, resort-style community pool surrounded by palm trees. It even has an attached cafe serving food and drinks, so you can easily spend all day lounging.
- **Private Beach Access:** As a guest in Seacrest Beach, you don't have to hunt for public parking. You get access to a private, pristine white-sand beach right across the street.
- **The 30A Lifestyle:** Hop on the provided bikes and within two minutes you're at the community pavilion. Every night there's live music, pizza, ice cream, coffee, and donuts. If you want to go a little further, you can ride right into Rosemary Beach for world-class dining.
