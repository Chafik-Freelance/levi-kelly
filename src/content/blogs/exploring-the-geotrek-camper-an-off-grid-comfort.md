I have always wondered what van life is actually like. Is it as glamorous as Instagram makes it look, or is it basically just a glorified tent on wheels?

To find out, my wife Savannah and I headed out to the Rocky Mountain National Park area to test out a brand new, custom 2025 all-wheel-drive Sprinter van built by the team at **Geotrek**. They build incredible adventure vans in Colorado, and after spending a few days off-grid in their "Albatross" layout, I can honestly say: van life gets better and better every single year.

Here is a look inside the rig that might just convince you to hit the road.

## The Exterior: Built for True Adventure

First off, this thing looks incredible. It sits up high—about 10 feet tall thanks to a lift kit and rugged all-terrain tires—so you can take it off-roading if you want to. But the real magic of this exterior is the functionality [[02:05](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8TdpfSRlJs&t=125)].

- **The Power System:** Up on the roof, there is 400 watts of solar, a Starlink dish for Wi-Fi anywhere, and an EcoFlow system holding 10 kW of battery power. You can charge it via solar, the alternator while driving, or a direct shore-power plug. I had the AC running, and after 24 hours off-grid, the battery was still at 74% [[10:52](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8TdpfSRlJs&t=652)]!
- **The Easy-Deploy Awning:** This is easily Savannah’s favorite feature. The awning cranks out super easily, and the legs attach to the side of the van using powerful magnets so you don't even have to put them on the ground [[02:29](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8TdpfSRlJs&t=149)].
- **The Outdoor Shower:** Open up the back doors, and you'll find a built-in showerhead that hooks up to the 25-gallon hot water tank. You can even attach a magnetic curtain for privacy when showering at a campground [[04:27](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8TdpfSRlJs&t=267)].

## Inside the "Albatross" Layout

Stepping inside the sliding door, the first thing I noticed is the headroom. I’m 5'10" and had plenty of space to stand up comfortably [[06:37](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8TdpfSRlJs&t=397)].

### The Kitchen

The galley features a gorgeous bamboo countertop, a deep sink with hot running water, and a blue fridge tucked underneath. Geotrek really thought of everything here: the upper cabinets lock with the push of a button so your plates don't fly everywhere while driving, and they even managed to fit a microwave up there [[07:43](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8TdpfSRlJs&t=463)].

They also added a really clever gray water system. You have a tank for national parks, but with a quick brass fitting swap, you can just drain the water directly onto the ground if you are somewhere where that's allowed [[08:11](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8TdpfSRlJs&t=491)].

### The Living & Dining Space

Behind the kitchen is a padded bench (which hides an emergency transportable toilet!). Next to the bench, a sturdy dining table swivels out from under the bed. It’s perfect for eating or setting up a laptop to get some work done [[08:31](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8TdpfSRlJs&t=511)].

When you aren't at the table, the front driver and passenger captain chairs actually swivel completely backward to face the interior of the van, adding even more living room seating [[11:35](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8TdpfSRlJs&t=695)].

### The Bedroom & Gear Garage

In the back is the main sleeping area, measuring 74 by 68 inches. It fit Savannah and me incredibly well [[09:49](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8TdpfSRlJs&t=589)]. Above the bed is a control screen showing your battery levels and solar input, reading lights with built-in USB chargers, and the dial for the diesel heater (perfect for winter camping) [[10:16](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8TdpfSRlJs&t=616)].

But the best part about the bed is what’s underneath it. The bed platform sits 36 inches high, creating a massive "gear garage" in the back of the van. It is the perfect size for storing mountain bikes and luggage completely out of your living space [[03:37](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8TdpfSRlJs&t=217)].

## The Verdict

If you've been on the fence about van life, now is the time. With Starlink Wi-Fi, air conditioning, massive battery banks, and a running hot shower, you are barely "roughing it." Geotrek builds everything in-house, from their cabinets to the electrical components, and it truly shows in the quality.
