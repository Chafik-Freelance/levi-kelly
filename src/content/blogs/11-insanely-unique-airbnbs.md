I recently posted a question on my YouTube channel asking what you guys considered to be a realistic "low-budget" vacation stay. While some of you asked for spots under $100 a night (which is pretty tough to pull off in today’s economy!), most of us agreed: **$200 a night out the door** is the sweet spot.

So, I scoured the internet and found 11 of the coolest, most unique Airbnbs across the country that you can book for a full weekend (Friday through Sunday) for $400 or less—including cleaning and Airbnb fees. Let's take a look at some of the highlights from the list!

## 1. The Hidden Pines (Franklin, NC)

**Price:** ~$331 for the weekend
When I saw this tiny house, I was shocked. Tucked away privately in the woods, the exterior is incredibly well done, but the interior blew me away. Bright white shiplap, tons of windows, a full kitchen, and an air conditioning unit. With a 4.94 rating out of 51 reviews, this is a budget-friendly tiny home done absolutely right.

## 2. The Highlands Treehouse (Buncombe County, NC)

**Price:** ~$319 for the weekend
If you want a true, suspended-in-the-air adventure, this is it. It’s a literal treehouse complete with a rope bridge! It sits incredibly high up in the trees. While the interior is more rustic and simple, you are paying for an unforgettable, unique experience. (And bonus: it looks pet-friendly!)

## 3. English Cottage in the Ozarks (Arkansas)

**Price:** ~$400 for the weekend
This one literally blew my mind. The landscaping alone is worth the price of admission. With a perfect stone exterior, a private pond, an outdoor grill, and a fireplace, this is the quintessential picture-perfect Midwest cottage. The interior features a farmhouse sink, beam ceilings, and sleek black cabinetry. It’s immaculate and heavily reviewed.

## 4. The Pink Airstream Oasis (Phoenix, AZ)

**Price:** ~$250 for the weekend
An Airstream for under $250 for the weekend is already a steal. But this one? It comes with a *cold plunge and a full pool*. The exterior is painted a vibrant pink, and the interior has been completely remodeled with an arched doorway, a smeg fridge, a mini wood-burning stove, and even a bathtub under a skylight.

## 5. Central Point Flower Farm (Central Point, OR)

**Price:** ~$370 for the weekend
I actually thought this listing was fake at first because it looks too good to be true. You are staying right on an active flower farm in Oregon. The interior feels incredibly luxurious—the kitchen alone has a $5,000+ range. With details like french wood doors and high-end brass fixtures, this place boasts the aesthetic of a $700/night stay.

## 6. Sun Grey Cabin Among the Stars (Westcliffe, CO)

**Price:** ~$377 for the weekend
Out west in beautiful Colorado, this cabin sits alone with zero obstructed views. No trees, no neighbors—just endless mountain, grassland, and sky. If you want absolute peace, quiet, and some of the best stargazing in the country, this cozy cabin with a gas fireplace and sleeping loft is perfect.

## 7. The Casita Nest (Austin, TX)

**Price:** ~$343 for the weekend
Located right in Austin, this backyard tiny house features a highly unique modern aesthetic. Surrounded by concrete, metal, clean lines, and architectural landscaping, it offers a totally different vibe than the farmhouse or cabin styles. Bright, white, and highly functional!
